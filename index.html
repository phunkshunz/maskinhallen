Install

Three steps.

Takes about a minute.

Download

Get the zip for Mac, Windows or Linux. Paid machines arrive by email and on your Payhip receipt.

Run the installer

Mac: open Terminal and run bash ~/Downloads/NAME/installera.command. Windows: right-click installera.bat → Run as administrator. Linux: run bash ~/Downloads/NAME/installera-linux.sh.

Rescan

Restart your DAW or rescan plugins. Bitwig, Ableton Live, Logic, FL Studio, Reaper and others all work.

About

Built in a
shipyard.

In the 90s, Stockholm's heaviest techno club was in a rattling shipyard. It was called Docklands.

That's where I learned how techno should feel: the bass that makes the steel plates rattle, the floor that sweats, and the rhythm that drags behind, stumbles, and still always lands on the one.

Someone once said my music sounds like a damp concrete floor. I took it as a compliment.

I have spent twenty years working with my hands on machines. Now I build the ones I always wanted in my studio.

Phunkshunz AUDIO is a name I have carried since I was a kid. It is a play on "function": machines that do one thing, and do it hard.

FAQ

Good to know.

Anything else? Ask through the store and the person who built the machines answers.

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 11 or later (Intel or Apple Silicon), 64-bit Windows 10/11, or 64-bit Linux (x86_64). Any host that loads VST3, AU or LV2.

Are the free ones really free?

Yes. BETONG and GLAPP are full versions. No email, no time limit, no noise.

Do they run on Windows?

Yes. Every machine comes for Mac and 64-bit Windows 10/11. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.

Is the interface in English?

Yes. The machines have Swedish names on the panel, but one click on SV | EN in the logo switches every label, menu and tooltip to English. Your choice is remembered across all machines. Preset names stay Swedish on purpose.

Do they run on Linux?

Yes. Every machine comes as VST3 and LV2 for 64-bit Linux (x86_64). The installer puts them in ~/.vst3 and ~/.lv2. Works in Bitwig, Reaper, Ardour and other Linux hosts.

How do I pay?

Card or PayPal through Payhip, in euro. VAT is handled automatically. You get the download link straight away. The code jim.paa gives 50% off single machines. The bundle is already at its launch price, no code needed.

Do I get updates?

Yes. New versions of what you bought are free. Download again from your Payhip receipt or the email Payhip sent you.

Do they work together?

They are built as one system. GLAPP drives BETONG, GRUND sits under it, FASFEL knocks above it, KULVERT puts it all in a basement, and KOLV conducts the lot.
