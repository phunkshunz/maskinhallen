The machine

What SMUTS does

Throw a drum loop at SMUTS, or a folder of a thousand. It finds every hit in three frequency bands, cuts each slice just before the attack on a zero crossing, works out the tempo and grid, and sorts the slices: kicks, hats, claps, percussion, tones, noise.

Then it builds. BUILD BEAT picks slices by quality and places them on a grid that drags and swings, with the kick nailed down so the floor stays solid. Six macros move the whole machine at once, and since 4.4 they can move by themselves in slow arcs over 3 to 16 bars.

Inside

Features

Chop and sort anything

Three-band onset detection with an adaptive threshold, slices that start on zero crossings, and sorting that hears a hat on a kick tail as a hat. HACKA I TAKT chops strictly in time, one slice per 16th.

Beats that drag and interlock

FRIKTION pulls hits late around a solid kick. Loops of different lengths interlock and answer each other, then drift out of phase and come back. GROOVE, KNASIGT, BUSS and GULD shape the engine room.

Six macros that move

SMUTS, SLÄP, STÖK, RYMD, KROSS and SVEP each move several controls at once, on top of your settings. RÖRELSE modulates them slowly in time so the beat keeps breathing.

MUTATE and undo

MUTERA lets two or three unlocked tracks take the roll's changes right away. Lock what you love, roll the rest, undo what you do not.

Whole libraries in one go

Import entire sample libraries. SMUTS listens to all of it and keeps the best slices of every kind ready for the next beat.
